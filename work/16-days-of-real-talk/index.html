Brief

A live client project for Socially Responsive Design at UTS. The Women’s and Girls’ Emergency Centre (WAGEC) asked our studio to launch its We Need To Talk cards to university students during the 16 Days of Activism against gender-based violence.

Almost half the students we surveyed had seen or experienced sexism at uni in the past year.

Response

We redesigned the deck as 30 pocket-sized cards, colour-coded by topic so students choose how deep to go. We used speech bubbles as the main device to show that not every opinion has to be fully formed to be part of the conversation.

Role

I worked on research and positioning, project management and the kickoff workshop, and designed and built the landing page in Webflow.

Printed cards. Card questions are by WAGEC. I set the card typography, shared the layout with Rachel, made the majority of the speech-bubble shapes and did the colours, textures and shadows. Teammates made the final artwork.

Process

  1. OBSERVED · DECK TEST · W3

    The original cards felt meaningful but too formal, large and academic, especially in a second language.

  2. DECIDED

    We made the cards smaller and type‑led, with clarity and readability as the priority.

  3. TYPOGRAPHY · MINE

    I set the card typography in Apercu Condensed and Work Sans, balancing clarity with warmth. I made the majority of the speech-bubble shapes and did the colours, textures and shadows. Teammates made the final artwork.

  4. WEB · MINE

    I designed the landing page, the campaign’s hub where students download the digital cards and find WAGEC resources. Ownership could not transfer, so WAGEC rebuilt it from my handover style guide and assets.

  5. REELS · CREATIVE DIRECTION MINE

    Student interviews paired with anonymous submissions, so students can take part safely.

  6. POSTERS

    By Angel, Helen, Seoyun & Nicole, using my type, shapes and textures. Campaign copy by Carmen, freelance copywriter.
