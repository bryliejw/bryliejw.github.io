Provenance & caveats
  • All options are proposed, not decided. Project titles, subtitles, years, roles and quotes come from the submitted taster folio (PDF pp 01–15). No new descriptive copy about the work.
  • Keyword links between projects are approximated from the p01 map and each project page’s labels. Confirm before the build.
  • Reference behaviour is from the earlier analysis of duda.works, Maria Vasilyeva, Gravity, Kavieng Cheng and Gionata Nese, plus A1 Gallery captures of similar folios (Gev Design, Kus, JARCOS, Michaël Bernard, Regis Biron, Primary Works).
  • Frames are scaled live prototypes. Final spacing follows the chosen type system at full width.
  • Drawings are colour-to-alpha cutouts from your Symbiosis process scans. Test footage shows testers from behind only.
  • Timeline positions within a year are spacing, not dates. New options leave freelance client names off until permission is confirmed.
